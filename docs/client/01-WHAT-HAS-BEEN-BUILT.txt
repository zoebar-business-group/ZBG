WHAT HAS BEEN BUILT

Prepared: 4 September 2026
Status: complete and ready for your final content review before the main domain


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN ONE PARAGRAPH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The site is finished and working. Twenty-two visitor-facing pages are live —
plus one more for every lot you publish — and every link on the site resolves;
nothing 404s. It is built as a buyer's tool rather than a brochure:
specification tables a green-coffee buyer can actually read, a lot record
system with QR codes that resolve to the individual coffee in front of them,
seven reference guides on the trade itself, and a company record where every
published fact is one you confirmed. It loads fast, works without JavaScript,
passes a full accessibility audit with zero violations, and is structured so
search engines and AI assistants can quote it accurately.

1. THE PAGES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOMEPAGE
  A single narrative in eleven chapters, opening on deep emerald. Origin,

THE COFFEE — /coffee
  The offer, as a specification surface. Confirmed product identity (origin,

AMARO — /amaro
  The origin page. What Amaro is, where it sits, how it grows coffee, and the

  recently named Koore Zone, and is not administratively part of the Sidama

  category. That accuracy is a genuine ranking advantage: almost nobody else
  states it correctly.

PROCESS — /process
  Cherry to container in seven stages: intake, sorting, washed and natural

  happens, who does it, and what is recorded at each stage.

QUALITY — /quality

  and the cup evaluation, what each measures, the seven points at which
  quality is fixed or checked between cherry and container, and who controls

2. THE TRUST RULE — HOW IT IS ENFORCED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your Foundation Brief says: "We would rather say 'we are verifying that
information' than provide an unsupported claim."

That is not a writing guideline on this site. It is built into the structure:

  - Every company and coffee fact lives in one file. Nothing about Zoebar is
    typed directly into a page, so one fact cannot say two different things in
    two places.
  - Any fact you have not confirmed is held as empty rather than filled with a
    plausible value. There are no "typical" altitudes, no representative
    cupping scores, no industry-average lead times anywhere on this site.
  - The machine-readable data that search engines and AI assistants read is
    generated by stripping out everything unconfirmed before it is written. An
    invented value cannot reach it even by mistake.
  - Following your feedback of 4 September, an unconfirmed field is now hidden
    from visitors rather than labelled, and lot-specific measurements are
    labelled "Confirmed per lot".

The practical consequence: every number published on this site is a number you
stated. That is the entire commercial proposition, and it is why the site can
be checked against the sack.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE LOT AND QR SYSTEM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each lot of coffee gets its own permanent page carrying its identity (lot
number, origin, harvest year, process, altitude), its quality record (grade,
screen size, cupping score, moisture) and its commercial detail (packing,
quantity, availability), plus the producers who grew it where they have given
consent to be named.

  - Each lot page generates its own QR code, downloadable as SVG or PNG, to
    print on the sack or sample bag. Scanning it opens that lot's page.
  - The printed reference and the web address are the same string, in a fixed
    format (for example amaro-washed-2026-01), checked automatically before
    it can be saved — because once it is printed on a sack it is permanent.
  - Lots are created and edited by you, in a content Studio at /studio. No
    developer is needed to publish a lot. The site updates within seconds of a
    lot being published.
  - Any field left blank stays blank. The system will not invent a figure to
    complete a lot record.
  - Following your feedback of 4 September, a lot is a demonstration record
    unless deliberately marked otherwise. A demonstration lot carries a clear
    banner, is kept out of search results and out of machine-readable product
    data, and does not present itself as something a buyer can order.

4. ENQUIRIES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The quote and sample forms deliver by email through Resend, with:

  - A notification to Zoebar and an automatic acknowledgement to the buyer.
  - Spam protection: a hidden honeypot field, Google reCAPTCHA scoring, and a
    rate limit per email address and per IP address.
  - Server-side validation, and every submitted value escaped before it
    reaches an email, so a form cannot be used to inject content.
  - An optional log to a Google Sheet as a backup record.
  - A WhatsApp route alongside the form.
  - If the mail service is not configured, the page says so honestly rather
    than accepting enquiries that would silently go nowhere.

Not yet done: a live end-to-end test with the real production mail and
reCAPTCHA keys. Worth doing once before the main domain goes live.

5. FINDABILITY — SEARCH ENGINES AND AI ASSISTANTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Every page carries a unique title, description and canonical address.
  - Machine-readable structured data throughout: the organisation, the
    website, the origin as a place, products, articles, FAQs, the founder as a
    person, and breadcrumb hierarchy on every page. All of it derives from the
    one company record, so it cannot drift from what the page says.
  - An XML sitemap that lists only pages that exist and are meant to be
    indexed, with real revision dates.
  - A robots file with its reasoning written into it, and an llms.txt for AI
    assistants.
  - A 1200x630 social sharing card, generated at build time.
  - Pages that exist but have nothing verified to say are deliberately kept
    out of search until they do. Shipping a page and indexing it are separate
    decisions here.
  - Content is written answer-first, in complete self-contained sentences, so
    an AI assistant quoting the site quotes something accurate and attributed.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DESIGN AND BRAND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Two densities, deliberately. Story pages (home, Amaro, producers, founder)
    open on deep emerald and are spacious. Specification pages (coffee,
    process, quality, lots) are light, dense and scannable. This reconciles
    the creative direction with the brand guideline rather than compromising
    between them.
  - Brand palette throughout: Alabaster, Deep Emerald, Earth Black, warm sand,
    sage and gold.
  - The Zoebar symbol is used exactly as supplied, in both fills. "Business
    Group" is never dropped from the wordmark.
  - Typography is set to Canela Deck as the display face, with a close
    substitute rendering until the web licence is in place — the swap is one
    line and needs no redesign.
  - Motion is restrained: content is in the page before any script runs, and
    everything respects a visitor's reduced-motion setting.
  - Specifications are always real tables, never pictures of tables, so they
    can be read by screen readers, copied, and indexed.

7. PERFORMANCE, ACCESSIBILITY AND QUALITY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Measured on a production build, not estimated:

  - Almost everything is pre-rendered as finished HTML at build time and
    served as a static file. Only four routes are generated on request: the
    quote form, the thank-you page, the QR code download, and the internal
    route that refreshes the site when you publish a lot.
  - Largest content paint: 260ms at worst on a local build. Layout shift:
    zero, on every page.
  - Page weight was cut from 357KB to 288KB by removing font data that was
    downloaded but never used. That figure was measured before the latest
    photographs were placed; pages carrying a photograph are heavier by the
    size of that image, which is normal and is the right trade.
  - Accessibility: from 399 issues down to zero, across WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2
    at levels A and AA. Re-confirmed after the latest changes: zero violations
    on all twenty pages tested.
  - No horizontal scrolling at any width from 360px to 1920px.
  - No console errors anywhere.
  - The site renders and reads correctly with JavaScript switched off.
  - An automated verification harness lives in the repository and runs 302
    checks across the site. Current state: 302 passed, 0 failed.

8. WHO OWNS AND EDITS WHAT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
YOU EDIT DIRECTLY, WITHOUT A DEVELOPER:
  - Lot records, including creating, editing, marking available or contracted,
    and switching a lot from demonstration to live. Via the Studio at /studio.
  - Producer profiles and their consent status, in the same place.

WE UPDATE FOR YOU (each is a small, contained change):
  - Company facts: specifications, commercial terms, legal details.
  - Page copy and the founder story.
  - Photographs.
  - Journal entries and guides.

Because every company fact lives in one place, a confirmed specification
appears on the coffee page, the quality page and every lot record at the same
moment. There is no risk of one page saying something different from another.
