1. QUALITY PAGE — KEPT, AND MADE EVIDENCE-LED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
You asked: keep the page; present grade, screen size, cupping score, moisture
and similar as lot-specific information ("Confirmed per lot") rather than as
permanent Zoebar specifications; and hide anything genuinely not yet available
rather than showing multiple "Being Verified" labels publicly.

DONE. Three separate changes:

a) The lot-specific specifications are back on the page, and they are labelled
   for what they are. The quality specification on /quality and /coffee now
   lists:

       Grade ................ Confirmed per lot
       Screen size .......... Confirmed per lot
       Cupping score ........ Confirmed per lot
       Defect count ......... Confirmed per lot
       Moisture content ..... Confirmed per lot

   These read as lot-specific measurements, not as standing claims about
   Zoebar coffee. A buyer sees that the figures exist and that they belong to
   the lot in front of them.

b) "Being verified" no longer appears anywhere on the public site. We checked
   every page: the count is zero. A field that is genuinely not confirmed yet
   is now simply not shown. It reappears on its own, on every page at once,
   the moment a real value is entered — nothing has to be un-hidden by hand.

   Fields now hidden until confirmed: varieties, packing, minimum order
   quantity, lead time, Incoterms, port of loading, inspection,
   certifications.

c) Because the whole commercial table would otherwise be blank, the commercial
   terms block on /coffee now carries one sentence in place of a column of
   empty rows:

       "Packing, minimum order quantity, lead time, Incoterms, port of
        loading, inspection and certifications are agreed against the contract
        and confirmed in writing on the offer. They are published here once
        they are fixed."

   Each row reappears individually as its value is confirmed.

d) The closing section of /quality was rewritten to match. It was headed "An
   empty field is a statement", which no longer describes how the page
   behaves. It now reads "A figure belongs to a lot", and makes the
   commercially stronger argument: most offer sheets carry one grade and one
   cupping score for a whole origin, and a buyer cannot check any of them
   against the coffee they are actually being sold.

IMPORTANT, AND UNCHANGED: nothing is ever filled with a plausible or
"industry typical" number. Hiding a field and inventing one are different
things, and only the first has been done.

5. QR / LOT SYSTEM — THE EXAMPLE LOT IS NOW UNMISTAKABLY A DEMO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
You asked: make it very clear that the current example lot is a demo/test lot
and not a live commercial offer.

DONE. A lot is now explicitly marked as either a demonstration record or a
real one, and a demonstration lot behaves differently in six ways:

  1. A banner sits above the record: "Demonstration lot — not a live
     commercial offer", explaining that the figures are an example of the
     fields a real lot carries and that none of them should be read as a
     specification, an availability or a price.
  2. The page heading above the lot number reads "Demonstration lot · not a
     live offer" instead of the origin and harvest year.
  3. Availability reads "Not offered — demonstration record" instead of
     "Available".
  4. The closing call to action reads "Ask about real lots" and routes to the
     general enquiry, instead of "Enquire about Lot 042".
  5. It is kept out of search results entirely, and out of the machine-readable
     product data that search engines and AI assistants read. A demonstration
     record cannot be picked up and presented as a live offer.
  6. In the lot index it carries a "Demonstration lot" badge rather than an
     availability status.

The QR code still works and still opens the page, so you can test a scan end
to end. The download note tells you not to print it on a sack.

SAFE BY DEFAULT. Any lot is treated as a demonstration lot unless someone
deliberately turns that off. An existing lot record — including the one you
reviewed — is automatically a demo without anyone touching it. Publishing a
real lot is a conscious act, and the failure mode is a caption on a real lot
rather than a demonstration record going out as a live offer.

WHEN REAL LOTS ARE READY: in the content Studio, switch "Demonstration lot"
off on that lot. Every one of the six behaviours above reverses at once, and
the lot index becomes visible to search. Real lots should only be switched
over once their figures are confirmed.

Where to look:  /lots  and any lot page
